Pressure is mainline’s side island. A black pill sits on the left edge, springs open when the pointer hits the middle, and keeps one live activity in view. Same idea as a dynamic island — made for the Mac, not by Apple.

live activities ~

the compact pill shows whichever one matters most. hover the left edge and the card opens with the rest.

highest

timer

1 · 5 · 15 · 25

start, pause, or cancel. when it hits zero the island pops open on its own, then can fold back up.

running

stopwatch

lap

start, stop, lap, and reset. the pill keeps the current time while it’s running, plus the last lap on the card.

music

now playing

live

music and spotify. title, artist, play, pause, previous, next. hidden when neither app is running or nothing is playing.

battery

or power

percent, charging, on battery, and a low-battery state. a desktop mac with no battery says power / ac instead of inventing one.

calendar

today

the next event today. tap it and calendar opens. if access is denied, or the day is clear, the row stays hidden.

memory

fallback

one live activity so the pill is never empty. percent, free memory, and a single auto-close switch. the rest of the settings stay in the settings window.

get it on your mac ~

drag it in, then right-click open. the build is ad-hoc signed and not notarized, so that first open needs the extra click.

  1. 01

    open the disk image

    download Pressure.dmg and drag Pressure into applications.

  2. 02

    right-click, then open

    if macOS says the developer can’t be verified, right-click Pressure in applications and choose open.

  3. 03

    find the left edge

    move the pointer to the vertical middle of the left edge. the pill morphs into the island.

requires macOS 13 or later

menu-bar app. no dock icon. the island stays on the left edge across spaces.
