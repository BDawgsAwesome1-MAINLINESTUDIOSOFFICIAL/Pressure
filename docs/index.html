macOS memory guardian

Pressure

Watches your Mac’s memory and closes the background apps you don’t need before macOS force-quits them.

A black pill sits on the left edge of the screen. Move the pointer there and it opens into controls for auto-close, warnings, and launch at login.

Download Pressure.dmg
  1. Open the disk image and drag Pressure into Applications.
  2. Open Pressure from Applications. If macOS says the developer can’t be verified, right-click the app and choose Open.
  3. Move the pointer to the middle of the left edge. The Hub opens there.

Requires macOS 13 or later. This build is ad-hoc signed and not notarized, so the first open needs the right-click step above.
